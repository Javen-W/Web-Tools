    いえの　前で　工事を　して　います。
    Points: 0 / 1
    Correct Answer Answer 1: こうじ is correct. Question Translation 「いえの　前で　工事を　して　います。」 → "They are doing construction in front of the house." Explanation The kanji 工事 means "construction" or "roadwork" and is read こうじ. In this sentence it describes the work being done in front of the house, so こうじ is the only natural reading. Other Options 2. こうごと – Not a standard word or reading for 工事, so it is incorrect. 3. こじ – Can be a reading for other words such as 孤児 (or 故事), but it does not match the kanji 工事 or the meaning "construction". 4. こごと – Usually written 小言(こごと) and means "scolding" or "complaint", which has different kanji and meaning from 工事.
