    1
    すぐに　集めて　ください。
    Points: 0 / 1
    Correct Answer Answer 3: あつめて is correct. It means "collect" or "gather." Question Translation Please collect (them) immediately. Explanation The kanji 集 reads as あつ (atsu) in the verb 集める (atsumeru - to collect/gather something). Note the tree (木) at the bottom and birds (隹) gathering on top. Why Other Options Are Incorrect 1. やめて (yamete) means "stop it" (from 止める/辞める). 2. きめて (kimete) means "decide" (from 決める). 4. とめて (tomete) means "stop" (movement) or "fasten" (from 止める/留める). 💡 Test-Taking Tip Be careful with transitive vs. intransitive verbs. 集める (Atsumeru) = You collect something. 集まる (Atsumaru) = People/Things gather naturally.
